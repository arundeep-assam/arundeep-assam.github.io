01 — About

Rooted in
Assam.

Based in Guwahati, Assam, I build Android apps that connect people to regional culture, language, and daily life. Every line of code carries Assamese pride.

I focus on modern Material Design and lightweight performance — whether it's morning news in Assamese, discovering ancient proverbs, or checking the weather during Bihu season.
